
## Questions & Answers

### What are the SQL isolation levels? How does PostgreSQL specifically implement them via MVCC (e.g. Read Uncommitted and Repeatable Read)?

**Key points:**
- Standard levels: Read Uncommitted, Read Committed, Repeatable Read, Serializable.
- PostgreSQL does not physically implement "Read Uncommitted". If you request it, you get "Read Committed".
- Due to Postgres's MVCC (Multi-Version Concurrency Control), "Repeatable Read" inherently prevents Phantom Reads, unlike standard SQL where phantoms are allowed at this level.
