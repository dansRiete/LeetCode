
## Questions & Answers

### How do you prevent lost updates in a highly concurrent payment scenario with 500 parallel requests? Would you use SELECT FOR UPDATE?

**Key points:**
- In a high-contention payment scenario, Optimistic Locking (`@Version`) will result in massive retry storms and exceptions.
- You should use Pessimistic Locking (`SELECT FOR UPDATE`), assuming the transaction is short. This queues the concurrent requests at the database level and prevents the lost update problem efficiently.
