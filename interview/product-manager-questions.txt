1. Translation of Requirements
An SPM's biggest pain point is often the gap between a business "need" and a technical "execution."

"Can you describe a time you took a vague stakeholder request and translated it into a concrete technical design?"

"How do you handle situations where a requested feature is technically unfeasible or would cause significant technical debt?"

"How do you communicate complex technical limitations to non-technical team members?"

2. Full-Stack Ownership & Reliability
Given the JD's emphasis on the "complete development life cycle" and "highly available cloud solutions":

"When designing a new Microservice using Spring Boot, how do you ensure it is 'highly available' once it hits production?"

"What is your approach to testing (unit, integration, and E2E) to ensure that product releases are stable and bug-free?"

"Describe your experience with CI/CD. How do you ensure that a code change doesn't break the existing user experience?"

3. Mentorship and Leadership
The JD explicitly mentions mentoring junior members and "training entry-level engineers."

"What is your philosophy on code reviews? How do you use them as a tool for mentoring rather than just gatekeeping?"

"If a junior developer is consistently missing deadlines or struggling with Spring/JPA best practices, how do you step in to help?"

4. Agile Process & Prioritization
"We operate in an Agile/Scrum environment. How do you handle 'scope creep' during a sprint?"

"If we have a critical production bug and a high-priority feature launch both due on Friday, how do you approach that trade-off?"

"What do you believe is the most important part of a successful Sprint Planning session?"

5. Data and Architecture
Because the role mentions both Relational and NoSQL (ElasticSearch, Mongo), they may ask about the "why" behind the tech:

"When would you recommend using a NoSQL solution like MongoDB or ElasticSearch over a standard Postgres/MySQL database for a new feature?"

"How do you ensure data integrity when working with complex data models across multiple microservices?"
