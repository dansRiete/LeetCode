
# Logic puzzles

## Questions & Answers

### Four people need to cross a bridge with one flashlight. They take 1, 2, 5, and 10 minutes respectively. The bridge holds 2 at a time. How do they cross in 17 minutes?

**Key points:**
- The trick is to send the two slowest people together so you don't incur their time penalties twice.
- Step 1: 1 and 2 cross (2 mins).
- Step 2: 1 returns with flashlight (1 min).
- Step 3: 5 and 10 cross together (10 mins).
- Step 4: 2 returns with flashlight (2 mins).
- Step 5: 1 and 2 cross together (2 mins).
- Total: 2 + 1 + 10 + 2 + 2 = 17 minutes.
